## Catching Mistakes Before Deploy

When a portfolio grows over years—especially when both you and AI assistants update it—small typos like a malformed date or a missing image path can easily slip in.

By defining strict schemas in `src/content.config.ts` and pairing them with `pnpm validate`, any invalid entry fails immediately in the terminal before it ever reaches production.
