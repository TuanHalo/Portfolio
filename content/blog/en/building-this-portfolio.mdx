## Why a Single Repository?

I wanted one place to store my bio, career timeline, project case studies, certificates, and writing—and have a fast website generated directly from those files.

## Key Decisions

1. **Markdown & YAML as the source of truth**: Every piece of data lives under `content/` and is validated with Zod at build time.
2. **Bilingual by default**: Routes under `/` serve English, while `/vi/` serves Vietnamese, with an automatic fallback notice when a translation isn't ready yet.
3. **Automated A4 Resume PDF**: Instead of maintaining a separate Word or Figma CV, Playwright prints the `/resume/` page to PDF during CI.
